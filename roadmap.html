PHASE 01 • MONTHS 1–6

FPGA Digital Twin, Emulation & EDA Bring-Up

ACTIVE EXECUTION

Synthesis, verification, and bring-up of the Dual-Core ATLNode processor matrix and 64-bit AXI4 crossbar on Xilinx Kintex-7/Zynq UltraScale+ FPGA platforms. Provisioning of enterprise EDA cloud instances via the MeitY ChipIN Centre. Full hardware emulation of asynchronous SPI bridges and peripheral digital twins.

PROTOTYPE:
FPGA Bitstream Validated
TOOLCHAIN:
ChipIN Cloud Grid
VERIFICATION:
Digital Twin Emulation
PHASE 02 • MONTHS 7–12

40nm Physical Design Closure & Pre-Silicon Sign-Off

DESIGN SIGN-OFF

Execution of full place-and-route (P&R), automated clock tree synthesis (CTS), power grid analysis, and multi-corner sign-off Static Timing Analysis (STA) across all operating corners (SS/FF/TT) utilizing target 40nm standard cell libraries and PDKs. Milestone execution synchronized under the MeitY Chips-DLI framework for pre-silicon closure.

TARGET PROCESS:
40nm CMOS Node
PHYSICAL CLOSURE:
PrimeTime Sign-off
FRAMEWORK:
ISM DLI Aligned
PHASE 03 • MONTHS 13–24

Silicon Tape-Out, MPW Shuttle & ATMP Packaging

SCHEDULED TAPE-OUT

Final GDSII database freeze and submission to tier-1 global pure-play foundry Multi-Project Wafer (MPW) shuttles on the 40nm CMOS node. Wafer dicing, post-silicon assembly, packaging into standard 64-pin QFN/BGA packages, and automated test equipment (ATE) characterization at accredited Indian ATMP facilities.

TAPE-OUT FORMAT:
GDSII Freeze & Hand-off
FOUNDRY RUN:
Global 40nm MPW Slot
PACKAGING:
QFN64 / BGA ATMP
